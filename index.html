The Monolith Risk Architecture

The natural successor to Jim Simons' Machine approach in his famous Medallion Fund, evolved for the age of AI. Having studied directly under Peter Cotton, and helping build his agentic prediction engine framework, and having studied Jim Simons' algorithm-ensemble framework, I've reconciled and combined the two to create The Monolith. The Monolith is a quasi-self-sufficient system akin to a synthetic brain, semi-capable of producing strategies and fully capable of producing measurables, scoring outcomes against predictors, self-correcting and pruning algorithms, and deploying/allocating strategies optimally. In short, it is semi-capable of generating neurons, and fully capable of strengthening the connections between neurons and removing inefficient connections.

The Stackelberg Flow Model (SFM) and Continuous Combination Engine (CCE) Systematic Execution

Automated delta-neutral trading and pricing infrastructure for threshold event contracts. Synthesizes order book microstructure, volume-threshold models, and Kelly sizing to exploit external institutional portfolio constraints. Built a Stackelberg Flow Model to model the relationship between small unconstrained leaders and large book-constrained followers, to capture the alphas that are (1) unreachable by larger firms and (2) manufactured by the constraints of larger firms. Paired with the Continuous Combination, where the Continuous Combination Engine identifies inventory imbalance in larger firms, and positional inefficiencies.
